# Chapter 10 Figure Inventory

Chapter 10 figures are either data plots generated from checked-in, cited CSVs or conceptual schematics without numeric axes:

- `ch10-cover-map`: Chapter cover map distinguishing the four evaluation objects, four judgments, matched baselines, deliberate stress-testing, and qualified program status.
- `fig-matched-complete-systems`: Matched complete-system workflow diagram comparing an AI-native design workflow with a conventional engineering baseline.
- `fig-benchmark-health-zero-point-contamination`: (a) agentic benchmark flaws transcribed from Zhu et al. 2025 (`data/datasets/chapter10-agentic-benchmark-flaws.csv`); (b) VeriContaminated Table I (`data/datasets/chapter10-verilog-contamination.csv`); generated by `data/datasets/generate_benchmark_health_ch10.py`.
- `tbl-llm-judge-blindspots`: Analytical taxonomy table (exposure column is an expectation, not a measurement) of hardware property classes (Classes I-IV), LLM-as-a-judge blind spots, and independent checking methods and their result limits.
- `fig-whac-a-mole`: Conceptual repair-and-rollback diagram (no measured values) showing candidate recurrence through physical-artifact signatures and the additional controller-state checks needed to identify a workflow cycle; generated by `data/datasets/generate_whac_a_mole_cycle_detection.py`.

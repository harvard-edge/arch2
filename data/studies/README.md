# Studies

These studies support the manuscript and the public data page. Chapters may read
their data directly, consume derived datasets, or include figures generated by
their scripts. The folder names preserve existing links; they do not determine
what the current evidence establishes.

## Evidence and verification

`measured` means an executed tool produced the recorded value; the tool may be a
parser or simulator rather than physical hardware. `mined` and `transcribed`
identify values obtained from an attributed source. Analytical calculations and
conceptual diagrams must remain distinguishable from observed results; they do
not supply missing empirical data. See the [data overview](../README.md#what-the-evidence-means).

The numbered-study provenance records use the `arch2-provenance/v1` schema:

| Field | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `claim` / `evidence_class` | The supported claim and how its evidence was obtained |
| `sources` / `datasets` | Source identifiers, data files, and row-level provenance fields |
| `produced_by` / `reproduce` | The generating script and reproduction command |
| `status` / `defects` | Recorded audit disposition and known problems |
| `verified` / `not_verified` | Checks performed and their explicit limits |

`verified` applies only to the checks described in the record. It is not an
independent certification of every row, a guarantee of generalization, or proof
that an entire toolchain was rerun. An empty `row_provenance` field needs an
explanation: an aggregate may be derived from a traceable CSV, while an empirical
row without any source remains unsupported.

The October 2026 raw-data audit independently recomputed the Studies 02, 06, and
08 headline aggregates. All 40 retained Study 06 stage-metric JSON hashes and
their 120 headline metric values matched the CSV. For Study 08, all 156 reference
and testbench pairs matched pinned upstream sources. Their adapted-testbench
hashes matched removal of waveform tasks, and all 338 mutant hashes matched
reconstructed edits. These checks did not reexecute the parser, OpenROAD,
Icarus, or Yosys.

The audit also identified reproduction and retention limits. Study 06's runner
and published CSV had diverged on the stdout-path column name; generator changes
must preserve compatibility with retained data. The package retains ODB hashes
without the ODB binaries. Eight Study 08 compact stdout entries retain a mismatch
witness but truncate the final count report. Historical generator hashes and
partial rerun records should remain distinguishable from later code corrections;
the study-level records carry the current details.

## Validator limits

`python3 data/validate_provenance.py` checks selected fabrication patterns,
provenance markers, and website asset references. It does not fetch and compare
every primary source. `python3 data/validate_figure_provenance.py` checks selected
figure declarations and source paths; an inventory entry is not proof
that the plot reads its declared input. Passing either validator is a useful check, not a factual
sign-off on the book.

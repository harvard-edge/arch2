## Editorial Notes for the Publisher

- Treat the owner locator as the preferred definition or principal discussion.
  Later locators are candidates for cross-references or subordinate entries,
  not duplicate definitions.
- Preserve distinctions that the manuscript relies on, especially artifact
  versus architecture result, execution failure versus candidate failure,
  architecture result versus program claim, and technical recommendation
  versus commitment.
- The Lighthouse cache study is prospective and unexecuted. The systolic-array
  study is a separate retained execution. They must not be indexed as one case.
- The Lighthouse thread and the Mobile XR subsystem case study are the same
  running example; the chapter sections that carry it now name the Mobile XR
  subsystem while the prose keeps the Lighthouse name. Index them together.
- Appendix A is a reviewer instrument, Appendix B is a survey map of
  AI-for-architecture work, and Appendix C is a chapter-principle digest.
  Appendix B owns only the latent architectural space map construct; otherwise
  the appendices do not own definitions established in the main text.
- Appendix C references use the principle numbering of its Core Principles
  section (principles 1 through 8), which preserves the numbering the former
  Design Principles appendix used.

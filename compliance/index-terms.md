## Editorial Notes for the Publisher

- Treat the owner locator as the preferred definition or principal discussion.
  Later locators are candidates for cross-references or subordinate entries,
  not duplicate definitions.
- Preserve distinctions that the manuscript relies on, especially artifact
  versus architecture result, execution failure versus candidate failure,
  architecture result versus program claim, and technical recommendation
  versus commitment.
- The Lighthouse cache study is prospective and unexecuted. The systolic-array
  study is a separate retained execution. They must not be indexed as one case.
- Appendix A is a reviewer instrument, and Appendix B is a chapter-principle
  digest. Neither appendix owns definitions already established in the main
  text.
